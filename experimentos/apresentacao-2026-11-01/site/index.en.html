Questions

Before you
take the next step.

What do I need to send for a quote?

Tell us about the property, what you want to present and which materials you already have. Floor plans, dimensions, photos and references help us assess the project. We agree on the materials needed before production begins.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

Both are set out in the proposal, based on the property and deliverables. Send us a message using the contact form so we can review your project.

Does the presentation replace an in-person viewing?

It complements the property listing. Dimensions, finishes, the property's condition and transaction details should be confirmed with the person responsible for the listing and the relevant documents.

Can I request more than one property?

Yes. We can discuss a proposal covering multiple properties. The production schedule and terms are agreed with our team.

Does the presentation reproduce every real-world detail?

The level of detail depends on the materials available and the agreed scope. The proposal states what will be represented, simplified or shown for illustration.
