Contact form privacy.

This notice describes the contact form on conectaimoveis3d.com and the information you choose to send through it.

What you send

The form asks for your name, email address and message. Your company name is optional. We use these details to review your enquiry and respond by email.

How your message reaches us

Firebase processes the submission, and Resend sends the message to Conecta’s contact mailbox, contact@conectaimoveis3d.com. The email address you provide is used when our team replies.

Correspondence may remain in the contact mailbox as part of the conversation with our team.

Technical records

The server also processes technical information to limit automated requests, prevent duplicate sends and record delivery status. These rate-limit and delivery records use technical metadata rather than the text of your message.

This form does not save your contact details or message in the website’s browser storage. Submitting it does not subscribe you to automated marketing.

Questions about your information

Write to contact@conectaimoveis3d.com to discuss the information you sent through this form.
